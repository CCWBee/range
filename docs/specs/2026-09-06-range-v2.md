# RANGE v2: flight model, mouse-aim instructor, textured aircraft, grounded scene

Spec for the second pass over RANGE, the self-contained three.js Typhoon demo. Written 2026-09-06.
The baseline is commit `9daab90` (the demo as the Codex session left it). Read `CLAUDE.md` and this
file in full before touching code. Design decisions here are settled; an implementer who finds one
unworkable writes down why in its report and takes the nearest workable option, it does not
re-open the design.

The user's brief, verbatim in substance: the flight model feels odd; fly towards the mouse like
War Thunder (a circle on screen, the plane pulls to it) with WASD still working; a small
instructor so you do not fall out of the sky; the aircraft must be a model with a skin, not a
monolithic grey shape; shadows and ground clutter so the scene stops looking blocky; keep the
prior approach (every asset modelled in Blender, textures generated, screenshots matched to the
five concept frames at 60 fps, one self-contained HTML file with no external assets); fix whatever
is found on the way and fill the gaps.

## 0. Conventions and hard rules

- World: +Y up. The runway runs along −Z; heading 000 is −Z (`heading = atan2(forward.x, −forward.z)`).
  The coast is at +X (east), the sea beyond it. The range pads are around (−1000, 0, −4200).
- Body: +X right wing, +Y up, −Z nose. Meshes are authored in Blender directly in this frame
  (Blender's own Z-up is ignored; the exporter copies coordinates verbatim, as `model_range.py` does).
- Aerodynamic sign convention used throughout the spec and the tests: `p` roll rate positive right
  wing down, `q` pitch rate positive nose up, `r` yaw rate positive nose right, `α` positive nose
  above the velocity vector, `β` positive wind from the right (nose left of velocity), `φ` bank
  positive right wing down. The mapping to the three.js body angular velocity `ω` is
  `q = ω.x`, `r = −ω.y`, `p = −ω.z`. Tests assert behaviour (heading increases when banked right),
  never raw signs.
- British English, no em dashes, anywhere: HUD copy, help panel, comments, docs, commit messages.
  Middle dot `·` remains the title separator.
- No emoji as icons, no pill or badge labels, no decorative dots, no rule lines along headings.
  Every HUD element is an instrument with a job.
- Nothing fetched from the network at runtime. `dist/index.html` must pass the existing build
  assertions (no external `src`/`href`, `connect-src 'none'`).
- One Blender instance (MCP on 127.0.0.1:9876, `tools/blender_call.py <script.py>` runs a script in
  it). Only one agent drives Blender at a time. Cycles bakes run in a background `blender.exe -b`
  process, never through the MCP socket (a long bake blocks Blender's UI thread and the socket
  times out mid-bake with no error).
- Keep `window.range.stage(name)`, `window.range.metrics()` and the five stage poses working. The
  visual gate depends on them.

## 2. Stream P: flight model (`physics.js`)

### 2.1 Why the old model felt odd

Attitude was driven by commanded rates with a first-order lag, lift acted on velocity with no
moments, so the nose swung without inertia, the ground was a flag with hacks, and rotation at
take-off was a pitch clamp rather than the elevons lifting the nose about the main wheels. Weight
comes from moments and inertia, so v2 is a rigid body with aerodynamic moments and a rate-command
fly-by-wire loop closing on top of it.

### 2.2 State

`Flight` keeps its public field names where they exist (the renderer and tests read them):
`position` (world m), `velocity` (world m/s), `attitude` (quaternion body to world), `throttle`
(0 to 1.12, 1.0 is maximum dry, above it reheat), `spool` (engine 0 to 1.12 following throttle
with lag), `gear` (commanded), `gearPosition` (0 up to 1 down), `brake`, `airbrake`, `grounded`,
`crashed`, `landed`, `alpha`, `load` (load factor n along body up), `bombs`, `rounds`,
`airborneTime`, `elapsed`, `mass`, `impact`.

New fields, all read by the renderer or HUD:

- `omega` Vector3, body angular velocity rad/s (mapping in section 0).
- `beta` rad, `mach`, `ias` m/s (indicated: `tas·sqrt(ρ/ρ0)`), `verticalSpeed` m/s, `reheat` 0 to 1
  (lit fraction, lags the throttle by 0.6 s), `stallSpeed` m/s (1 g, current mass), `buffet` 0 to 1,
  `condensation` 0 to 1, `stall` boolean, `crashReason` string, `stopped` boolean.
- `surfaces`: `{canardL, canardR, elevonL, elevonR, rudder, airbrake, nozzle, steering}`. Angles in
  rad, positive trailing edge down (canards: leading edge up positive), `airbrake` and `nozzle` 0
  to 1 (`nozzle` 1 is fully open, in reheat), `steering` rad nose wheel, positive right.
- `legs`: array `[nose, left, right]` of `{contact, compression (0 to 1), load (N), spin (rad/s)}`.
- `onGround` boolean: any wheel in contact.
- `basis()` unchanged: `{forward, up, right}` world unit vectors.
- `reset()`, `step(dt, cmd)` where `cmd = {pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, brake, airbrake}`: `pitch`,
  `roll`, `yaw` are the FCS demands in −1..1 (pitch positive pull, roll positive right wing down,
  yaw positive nose right), `throttle` is a rate −1..1 integrated at 0.40 per second (the old
  behaviour, keep it), `brake` and `airbrake` booleans. Also `setThrottle(value)` for staging.
- `bombStep(bomb, dt)` stays exported with the same contract (returns true on ground impact); add
  fin-stabilised drag so the bomb noses over: `velocity *= exp(−0.018·dt)` and a slow alignment of
  the bomb's reported `heading` to its velocity (the renderer orients the mesh from velocity).

### 2.3 Airframe numbers

Eurofighter Typhoon, single seat, four inert 250 kg practice bombs on wing pylons, wingtip pods.

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| mass with four bombs | 15 200 kg (each bomb release −250 kg; gun rounds ignored) |
| inertia Ixx (roll), Iyy (pitch), Izz (yaw) | 24 000, 105 000, 120 000 kg·m², scaled by mass/15 200 |
| wing area S, span b, mean chord c̄ | 51.2 m², 10.95 m, 4.7 m |
| thrust per engine | 60 kN dry at throttle 1.0, +30 kN reheat at 1.12 (linear between) |
| idle thrust | 4 % of dry at throttle 0 |
| thrust with altitude and Mach | × (ρ/ρ0)^0.75 × (1 + 0.25·M) dry, × (1 + 0.4·M) for the reheat part |
| spool | first-order lag, τ 1.6 s accelerating (2.4 s below 50 %), 1.0 s decelerating; `reheat` lights with τ 0.6 s once `spool > 1.0` |
| air density | ρ = 1.225·exp(−h/9500) |

### 2.4 Aerodynamics

Dynamic pressure `q̄ = ½ρV²`. Non-dimensional rates `p̂ = p·b/2V`, `q̂ = q·c̄/2V`, `r̂ = r·b/2V`
(guard V ≥ 5 m/s). Control deflections in rad: elevon `δe` (±0.44), aileron differential `δa`
(±0.35), rudder `δr` (±0.52). All coefficients are per rad.

Lift: `CL = 0.05 + 3.4·α` for |α| ≤ 0.32; beyond, `CL = sign(α)·max(0.6, 1.14 − (|α| − 0.32)·1.8)`.
Add `+0.35·δe`. Peak 1.14 at 18°, so the 1 g stall at 15.2 t is about 64 m/s (125 kt) and the
approach at 82 m/s (160 kt) sits at α ≈ 11°.

Drag: `CD = CD0·(1 + 2·s(M, 0.88, 1.10)) + k·CL²·(1 + 1.5·s(M, 0.88, 1.10))` with `CD0 = 0.021 +
0.025·gearPosition + 0.07·airbrake + 0.004·bombs`, `k = 0.16`, plus post-stall `+0.4·max(0, |α| −
0.28)`. `s(x, a, b)` is smoothstep.

Side force `CY = −0.85·β + 0.18·δr`.

Moments (body, aero convention):

- `Cl = −0.06·β − 0.32·p̂ + 0.10·r̂ + 0.11·δa`
- `Cm = −0.02 − 0.30·α − 7.5·q̂ − 1.2·δe − 0.35·s(|α|, 0.30, 0.45)·sign(α)`
- `Cn = 0.10·β − 0.28·r̂ − 0.02·p̂ − 0.09·δr + 0.012·δa`

Forces: lift along `right × velocityDirection` (the existing construction), drag along
`−velocityDirection`, side force along `right`, thrust along `forward`, gravity. Moments
`L = q̄·S·b·Cl`, `M = q̄·S·c̄·Cm`, `N = q̄·S·b·Cn`, mapped into ω components by section 0.
Integration: semi-implicit Euler at 120 Hz (the renderer's fixed step), `ω̇ = I⁻¹(M − ω × Iω)`,
attitude advanced by the quaternion of `ω·dt`, normalised. Clamp |ω| ≤ 6 rad/s. Structural failure
(`crashReason 'airframe'`) at n > 12 or n < −5.

Canards are inside `Cm`; their visible deflection is `canard = −0.6·δe`, and on the ground with
weight on wheels they droop to −0.35 rad (the Typhoon parks them leading edge down).

### 2.5 Fly-by-wire (always on, the airframe's own)

Gain-scheduled on dynamic pressure: `qn = clamp(q̄, 3000, 40000) / 15000`.

Pitch: demand `q_dem = pitch · q_max` where `q_max = min(9·g0/V, 1.2)` for pull and `min(3·g0/V,
0.6)` for push; α limiter `q_dem −= 6·max(0, α − 0.34)` and `q_dem += 6·max(0, −0.14 − α)`.
Controller `δe = −(0.9·(q_dem − q) + 1.2·∫(q_dem − q)dt) / qn`, integrator clamped ±0.3,
`δe` clamped ±0.44 and rate-limited to 3.5 rad/s. Nose up needs negative `δe`.

Roll: `p_max = 4.2 rad/s · clamp(V/120, 0.35, 1) · (1 − 0.5·bombs/4) · (1 − 0.6·gearPosition)`;
`δa = 0.25·(roll·p_max − p) / qn`, clamped ±0.35, rate 5 rad/s.

Yaw: `δr = yaw · 0.52 · clamp(1 − V/250, 0.15, 1) − 1.5·β`, clamped ±0.52. Nose wheel steering on
the ground `steering = yaw · clamp(0.6·(1 − V/40), 0.05, 0.6)` rad.

### 2.6 Wheels and ground

Three contact points in body coordinates: nose `(0, −1.83, −4.35)`, mains `(±1.15, −1.87, 2.6)`.
Ground height: 0 on pavement (|x| < 31 on the runway, or inside the apron and taxiway boxes
listed in `tools/model_range.py`), else terrain height from the shared function
`terrainHeight(x, z)` exported by `physics.js` (formula in section 3.4; the same function the
Blender script and the renderer use). Each wheel is a spring-damper along world up: `k` 260 kN/m
mains, 120 kN/m nose, damping ratio 0.7, travel 0.35 m. Contact force acts at the wheel point and
produces moments, so the aircraft settles nose-level on all three, lifts its nose about the mains
when the elevons say so, and can tip if abused. Rolling resistance μ 0.02 pavement, 0.08 grass;
brakes on mains μ 0.45 (wet concrete); lateral tyre force `−8·Fz·slipAngle` saturated at
`0.7·Fz`, nose wheel steered by `steering`. `gearPosition` scales the whole gear (retracted gear
gives no contact, and a fuselage touch is a crash).

Crash (`crashed = true`, `crashReason` set, velocity zeroed, aircraft left where it is):
`'impact'` any wheel contact with vertical speed above 5.5 m/s or wheel travel exceeded at speed;
`'gear'` contact with `gearPosition < 0.9`; `'attitude'` bank beyond 15° or pitch below −6° at
wheel contact, or the nose tip `(0, −0.3, −8)` or a wingtip `(±5.4, −0.1, 3.9)` below ground;
`'terrain'` on grass above 25 m/s; `'water'` position below sea level east of the coast;
`'airframe'` as above. `grounded` is all three wheels in contact for 0.2 s; `landed` is set on the
first touchdown after `airborneTime > 10`; `stopped` is `grounded && speed < 0.5`.

Gear moves in 2.6 s and can only be commanded when no wheel is in contact (the renderer already
enforces the key; the model enforces it too).

### 2.7 Derived cues for the renderer

`buffet = s(α, 0.24, 0.34) · clamp(q̄/8000, 0, 1)`; `condensation = s(α, 0.17, 0.30) ·
clamp((V − 80)/120, 0, 1)`; `stall = α > 0.34 && !onGround`; `surfaces.nozzle = clamp((spool −
0.85)/0.27, 0, 1)`; `surfaces.airbrake` follows the airbrake command with τ 0.5 s; wheel `spin =
groundSpeed / 0.31` while in contact, decaying with τ 4 s after lift-off.

### 2.8 Acceptance tests (`tools/test_flight.mjs`)

Run with `node tools/test_flight.mjs`; every assertion prints `PASS <name> <json>`. Keep the seven
existing tests, retuning their expected numbers to the new model, and add:

1. Take-off in reheat with a pull at 74 m/s: rotation between 65 and 80 m/s, main wheels leave
   the ground between 500 and 1100 m of run, positive climb, no crash.
2. 1 g stall speed at 15.2 t between 58 and 70 m/s (decelerate in level flight with the FCS
   holding α at its limit; record V when height starts falling faster than 3 m/s).
3. Roll rate: full roll input at 200 m/s clean reaches |p| ≥ 3.0 rad/s within 1 s; at 75 m/s with
   gear down it stays below 1.6 rad/s.
4. Energy: a held 6 g turn at 250 m/s for 10 s bleeds at least 15 m/s.
5. Wheels: parked with idle thrust the aircraft settles level (pitch within 1°) within 3 s and does
   not creep; full brakes from 60 m/s stop it inside 900 m.
6. Frame-rate stability: 60 Hz versus 120 Hz stepping differ by less than 5 m after 8 s of the
   take-off script.
7. Instructor tests (section 3.6), using `aimFromAngles`.
8. A gentle geared touchdown at 80 m/s, −2 m/s, on the runway centreline survives; the same at −7
   m/s crashes with `crashReason 'impact'`; the same with gear up crashes with `'gear'`.

## 3. Stream P: mouse-aim instructor (`control.js`)

### 3.1 Contract

```js
export class Instructor {
  constructor()            // mode 'assist' (default) or 'manual'
  mode                     // 'assist' | 'manual'
  state                    // {regime, bankDesired, stallGuard, alphaLimited, gLimited, active}
  update(dt, flight, aim, keys) // returns cmd for flight.step
}
export function aimFromAngles(flight, azimuth, elevation) // world unit vector, for tests and staging
```

`aim = {direction: Vector3 | null, active: boolean}`: `direction` is the world unit vector of the
camera ray through the on-screen cursor, `active` is false when the pointer is not locked (hands
off). `keys = {pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, brake, airbrake}` with the same meaning as `cmd`
(section 2.2), from the keyboard. `aimFromAngles` rotates the aircraft's world forward by
`azimuth` about world up (positive right) and then by `elevation` about the aircraft's right axis
(positive up); it is what the tests and the staging poses use in place of a cursor.

Throttle, brake and airbrake pass straight through from `keys` in both modes.

### 3.2 Assist law

Express the aim direction in body axes: `t = (tx, ty, tz)`, `tz` negative ahead. Lateral and
vertical errors `ex = atan2(tx, −tz)`, `ey = atan2(ty, −tz)` (rad), total angle off `θ`. Current
bank `φ = atan2(−right.y, up.y)`. Blend weight `w = s(θ, 0.12, 0.35)`.

Small-angle regime (weight 1 − w): bank to turn. `φ_des = clamp(6·ex, ±1.2)`; `p_dem = 4·(φ_des −
φ)`; `q_dem = 3·ey + (g0/V)·tan φ·sin φ` (the second term holds the nose in a banked turn, cap it
at 0.4 rad/s). A target below the nose is a push, never a roll to inverted.

Large-angle regime (weight w): roll so the target is above the nose, then pull. `φ_need =
atan2(tx, ty)`, `Δφ = wrap(φ_need − φ)`, `p_dem = 5·Δφ`, `q_dem = 2.5·θ·max(0, cos Δφ)` so it
never pulls the wrong way while still rolling.

Combine `p = (1−w)·p_small + w·p_large`, same for `q`; clamp `p` to 3.5 rad/s. Convert to FCS
demands `pitch = q_dem / q_max`, `roll = p_dem / p_max` using the same `q_max`, `p_max` formulas as
section 2.5 (export them from `physics.js` so the two files agree), clamped −1..1.

Instructor limits, tighter than the FCS: α cap 0.30 (`q_dem −= 8·max(0, α − 0.30)`), positive g
cap 8.

Keyboard on top: `roll += 0.8·keys.roll` (so A and D roll about the aim, and can hold the aircraft
inverted against the instructor), `pitch += 0.6·keys.pitch`, `yaw = keys.yaw`. Cursor at centre
(θ < 0.02): `φ_des = 0`, hold `ey`. Hands off (`aim.active` false or `direction` null): aim at the
level forward direction, which levels the wings and settles pitch.

### 3.3 Stall guard (the "do not fall out of the sky")

`vs = flight.stallSpeed`. Guard strength `g = 1 − clamp((ias − vs)/(0.10·vs), 0, 1)`
(1 at or below the stall, 0 above 1.10·vs). Positive `q_dem` is scaled by `(1 − g)`. When `g > 0.7`
or `α > 0.32`: `q_dem = min(q_dem, −0.15)` and `φ_des = 0` until `ias > 1.06·vs`. Report in
`state.stallGuard`. The guard is off with weight on wheels.

### 3.4 On the ground

Lateral error steers: `yaw = clamp(3·ex, −1, 1) + keys.yaw`. Pitch stays 0 below `0.9·Vr` with
`Vr = 1.12·vs`; at or above `Vr`, `pitch = clamp(8·ey, 0, 0.7)` when the cursor is above centre
(`ey > 0.02`) or `keys.pitch > 0`, so rotation is a deliberate action. After lift-off the flight
law takes over without a step (blend over 1 s on `airborneTime`).

### 3.5 Manual mode

`mode = 'manual'`: `pitch = keys.pitch`, `roll = keys.roll`, `yaw = keys.yaw`, nothing else. The
FCS limits still apply because they belong to the airframe. Toggle key is I (section 5.4).

### 3.6 Instructor tests

With `aimFromAngles`, 120 Hz, starting at 1000 m, 160 m/s, clean, throttle 0.8 unless stated:

- Aim 15° right for 8 s: heading increases by 20° to 90°, height within ±120 m, no crash, bank
  never beyond 75°.
- Start at 60° bank, aim centred: bank within 5° of level inside 4 s.
- At 75 m/s with aim 20° up for 10 s: no crash, α stays ≤ 0.34, `state.stallGuard` exceeds 0 at
  some point.
- Hands off (`active` false) for 20 s at 120 m/s: height changes less than 150 m, bank stays under 5°.
- Aim 12° below for 3 s: pitch falls, bank stays under 15°, load factor stays above −1.
- Aim 60° right for 6 s: bank exceeds 60° within 2 s, heading increases by at least 40°.
- Ground: from parked, throttle to reheat, aim centred: no rotation before `Vr`; aim raised 10°
  after `Vr`: airborne within 20 s.
- Landing: from 4 km out on the centreline at 300 m, 82 m/s, gear down, aim held on a point on the
  threshold: touchdown with vertical speed under 3 m/s, no crash, then brakes stop it within 1500 m.
- Manual mode: roll right 1 s then centre for 6 s: heading increases.

## 4. Stream B: Blender assets and textures

### 4.1 Library format (replaces the JSON-only export)

`tools/export_meshes.py` writes `assets/meshes.json` (manifest) and `assets/meshes.bin` (binary).
It reads UVs per loop (`loop_triangles[i].loops` → `uv_layers.active.data[loop].uv`), never per
vertex, and writes indexed geometry (welded on position, normal and UV).

Manifest:

```json
{
  "version": 2,
  "blender": "5.0.1",
  "materials": {"name": {"colour": [r,g,b], "metallic": 0.4, "roughness": 0.5,
                          "map": "jet_basecolor", "normalMap": "jet_normal", "ormMap": "jet_orm"}},
  "assets": {"asset": [{"material": "name", "vertexCount": n, "indexCount": m,
                        "position": byteOffset, "normal": byteOffset, "uv": byteOffset,
                        "color": byteOffsetOrNull, "index": byteOffset,
                        "bounds": [minx,miny,minz,maxx,maxy,maxz]}]},
  "pivots": {"jet_canard_l": [x,y,z], ...},
  "terrain": {"nx": 256, "nz": 256, "originX": -12000, "originZ": -14000, "spacing": 110,
              "heights": byteOffset}
}
```

Binary: positions, normals as Float32 ×3, uv Float32 ×2, colour (vertex AO, Uint8 ×3, normalised)
where present, index Uint32, terrain heights Float32 row-major (z outer, x inner). Every block is
4-byte aligned. Texture names are file stems under `textures/` (the renderer and `build.py` resolve
`.jpg` first, then `.png`).

### 4.2 The aircraft: a model with a skin

Rebuild the Typhoon in `tools/model_range.py` (the file is rewritten; keep its helpers where they
still serve). Reference silhouette: `concepts/ramp.png` and `concepts/cloud-break.png`. Length
15.96 m, span 10.95 m, height 5.28 m. Nose at z = −8.0, tail at +6.0, as now.

Parts, each its own asset with a pivot in `pivots` (pivot in body coordinates, the part is
exported relative to the origin so the renderer positions it at the pivot and rotates about it):

| asset | pivot / axis | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `jet_body` | origin | fuselage, wings, fin, intakes, canopy frame, wingtip pods, pylons, formation light strips, antennas, pitot |
| `jet_canopy` | origin | glass only, its own material `canopy` |
| `jet_canard_l`, `jet_canard_r` | hinge on the canard root, axis along the canard span | rotates by `surfaces.canardL/R` |
| `jet_elevon_l`, `jet_elevon_r` | hinge line along the wing trailing edge, inboard end | full-span trailing-edge flaperons, `surfaces.elevonL/R` |
| `jet_rudder` | hinge line up the fin | `surfaces.rudder` |
| `jet_airbrake` | hinge at its front edge on the dorsal spine | opens by `surfaces.airbrake·0.9` rad |
| `jet_nozzle_l`, `jet_nozzle_r` | nozzle centre | petal ring; the renderer scales the petal ring radially by `1 − 0.18·(1 − nozzle)` |
| `jet_gear_nose`, `jet_gear_main_l`, `jet_gear_main_r` | retraction pivot at the top of each leg | leg plus wheel, rotates from stowed to down by `gearPosition` (nose folds forward, mains fold inward) |
| `jet_gear_doors` | origin | door panels, opened by `gearPosition` as a group scale on the hinge |

Modelling bar: the fuselage is a lofted section series with a chine, a real intake bump beneath
the cockpit, blended wing roots, a canopy with a frame and a separate glass shell, exhaust cans
with visible petals and a dark interior, the vertical fin with a rudder split. Bevel or subdivide
edges that catch light. Budget 60 000 triangles for the whole aircraft. Judge it by viewport
screenshots from the five stage camera positions (MCP `get_viewport_screenshot`); the rear
three-quarter view must read as a Typhoon at a glance.

UV unwrap: Smart UV Project with 45° angle limit and 0.004 island margin on every jet part, packed
into one 0..1 atlas across all parts (the parts share the atlas). Canopy glass shares it too.

Bake (`tools/bake_jet.py`, run as `blender.exe -b assets/RANGE.blend -P tools/bake_jet.py`,
Cycles, OptiX, 64 samples, margin 8 px, 2048²): a node material that composes the skin, baked to
three atlases in `textures/`:

- `jet_basecolor` (JPEG q 90): RAF air superiority grey (BS 626, about sRGB 0.60 0.63 0.66) with
  panel lines from an edge/pointiness mask, rivet rows along seams, the existing `airframe.png` as
  micro grain at 8× tiling, low-visibility roundels on the wings and intake sides, a two-letter
  code on the fin, walkway stripes on the wing roots, exhaust soot aft of the nozzles and along the
  fuselage sides, grime streaks running aft from panel lines, darker radome and dielectric panels,
  dark titanium nozzle cans, black tyres and grey hubs on the gear.
- `jet_normal` (PNG): the bump of panel lines and rivets baked from the same masks.
- `jet_orm` (JPEG q 90): R = ambient occlusion (baked), G = roughness (paint 0.42, canopy 0.08,
  titanium 0.3, tyres 0.8), B = metallic (paint 0.35, titanium 0.9, tyres 0).

The materials in the manifest for the jet reference these three maps: `airframe`, `canopy`,
`titanium`, `rubber`. If the bake is still running when the exporter runs, the exporter still
writes the manifest and the renderer's loader falls back to flat colours for a missing map.

### 4.3 Scenery library

All authored in `tools/model_range.py`, each an asset in the library, vertex ambient occlusion

Terrain: heights come from `terrainHeight(x, z)` (section 3.4 of `physics.js`; B duplicates the
formula in Python exactly and asserts a few sample points against numbers listed in the script):

```
coast(z) = 1600 + 350·sin(0.0008·z) + 180·sin(0.0018·z)
inland(x, z) = −0.34 + max(0, |x| − 2200)·0.006·(0.6 + 0.4·sin(0.001·z))
             + 6·fbm2(x·0.0009, z·0.0009)·s(|x| − 700, 0, 1500)   (rolling moor, fbm2 in −1..1, seeded 7)
shore: for x in (coast − 250, coast): −0.34 − 6·((x − coast + 250)/250)²
sea: x ≥ coast: −7 − min(25, (x − coast)·0.01)
pavement footprints are flattened to −0.34 within 60 m of their edges (smoothstep)
```

`fbm2` is the same three-octave value noise in both languages (specify the hash so Python and JS
agree; test three sample points). The Blender terrain mesh is 256 × 256 samples at 110 m spacing,
exported as the height grid, not as triangles.

## 5. Stream R: renderer (`src/`)

### 5.1 Modules and bundling

`src/main.js` (bootstrap, loop, `window.range`), `src/loader.js` (manifest + binary → geometries,
textures with fallbacks), `src/world.js` (sky, atmosphere, ocean, terrain, lighting, scenery
placement, clutter instancing, lights), `src/aircraft.js` (assembles the parts, animates surfaces,
gear, nozzles, flames, condensation, lights), `src/effects.js` (bombs, gun, explosions, smoke,
spray, tyre smoke, wrecks), `src/camera.js`, `src/input.js` (keys, pointer lock, cursor),
`src/hud.js`, `src/audio.js`, `src/post.js` (bloom, final grade). ES modules with relative
imports; `tools/build.py` concatenates them in dependency order (strip `import`/`export`, wrap each
in a block that assigns its exports to a shared `RANGE` object, or any equivalent that keeps
`dist/index.html` a single inline script). The binary library is inlined as base64 in
`window.RANGE_BIN`; textures as data URIs keyed by stem. Target bundle under 30 MB; `build.py`
prints the byte count and fails above 40 MB.

### 5.2 Camera (the weight lives here)

Chase camera aligned to the aircraft, not to the mouse. Smoothed forward `fs` is a slerp toward
the aircraft's velocity direction (forward when V < 40 m/s) with τ 0.35 s; desired offset in that
smoothed frame is `−fs·(24 + 4·clamp(V/300, 0, 1)) + worldUp·6.5`. Filter the offset in the
aircraft's frame (critically damped, ω 6 rad/s) and then place it in world space, so translation
never stretches at speed but rotation lags. Camera up is `mix(worldUp, aircraftUp, 0.3)`. Look at
`position + fs·40`, plus 15 % of the angle toward the aim direction. FOV 50° to 58° with speed.
Load factor pulls the camera 0.35 m per g down and back; `buffet` adds 12 Hz noise of 0.06 m per
unit. Never below 2.7 m above ground. The pre-start pose stays the low three-quarter view. Key C
still toggles the wider view.

### 5.3 Cursor and aim

Pointer lock on canvas click once the sortie has started. Mouse deltas accumulate into a cursor
offset from screen centre (sensitivity 1.0 px per px), clamped to a circle of radius
`0.36·innerHeight`. Cursor to world: unproject the NDC point through the live camera each frame;
`aim = {direction, active: pointerLocked}`. `pointerlockchange` to unlocked while running pauses;
click resumes and relocks. The reticle is drawn at the cursor as a 26 px circle with a 2 px centre
mark; a second marker, a 10 px cross, sits on the projected nose direction; the flight-path marker
(a 14 px circle with 8 px wings) sits on the projected velocity vector. All three are plain 1 px
lines in the HUD's off-white, no fill, no glow.

## 7. Performance budget

At 1920 × 1080, pixel ratio 1, on the RTX 5070 Ti: draw calls ≤ 160, triangles ≤ 2.0 M, two
shadow passes, bloom at one third resolution. Vsync will show 6.94 ms at 144 Hz; the headroom
number is `stress(true)` (pixel ratio 2, four times the pixels) and must stay under 16.6 ms mean
at every stage. Report both.
